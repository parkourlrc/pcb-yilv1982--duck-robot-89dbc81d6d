1.0 SCOPE 
1.1 This specification covers performance, testing and quality requirements for Samtec’s SSW/TSW Series .100’’(2.54 
mm) .025’’ square post socket and terminal strip and SMH/TSW series SMT horizonal socket and terminal strip. All 
information contained in this specification is for a though-hole socket / though-hole terminal mated configuration 
unless otherwise noted. 
 
2.0 DETAILED INFORMATION 
 
2.1 Product prints, footprints, catalog pages, test reports and other specific, detailed information can be found at 
http://www.samtec.com/?SSW, https://www.samtec.com/products/smh and http://www.samtec.com/?TSW

4.5 Stencil Thickness: The stencil thickness is .006” (0.15 mm). 
 
4.6 Placement: Machine placement of the parts is strongly recommended. 
Note:  If the Locking Clip option (-LC) is used, manual placement will be required if the force needed to fully seat the 
connector exceeds the limit of the machine placement equipment.  For more information, please visit the Processing 
page on our website or contact Samtec’s Interconnect Processing Group at IPG@samtec.com. 
 
4.7 Reflow Environment:  Samtec recommends the use of a low level oxygen environment (typically achieved through 
Nitrogen gas infusion) in the reflow process to improve solderability. 
 
4.8 Hardware: Board-to-board standoffs are recommended to provide a robust mechanical connection. Samtec offers 
two different types:  
 
4.8.1 
Traditional Standoffs (SO) – Rigid design to statically support board-to-board applications.  See options 
here: SO - Board Stacking Standoff 
 
4.8.2 
Jack Screw Standoffs (JSO) – Serve same function as traditional standoffs but unique, nested 
construction facilitates the mating and unmating process.  This is especially helpful for multiple 
connector applications where the mating and unmating forces increase with the number of 
connectors used.  See options here: JSO - Jack Screw Standoffs 
 
4.9 Cleaning: Samtec, Inc. has verified that our connectors may be cleaned in accordance with the solvents and      
conditions designated in the EIA-364-11 standard.
